# RecoveryCase lifecycle

ReRoute moves a detected case through an explicit investigation and current policy evaluation before marking it eligible. Each selected Action requires a persisted business-owner approval, then moves the case to awaiting outcome, applies a 24-hour limit, and cannot exceed three customer contacts for the permanent case. Provider failures and help requests escalate to a human; a business owner may resume after another current policy evaluation. A matching signed Test Mode capture is provider-authoritative and records the Outcome while recovering the case. Stop conditions close the case permanently.
