What to watch

The product should guide you through this loop without a manual refresh.

1
Normal payments establish a baselineSentinel watches quietly in the background.
2
A cohort-level incident appearsThe detector is deterministic; AI does not open incidents.
3
Facts, AI and Policy stay separateHard declines can remove Retry before the model ever ranks actions.
4
The merchant approvesAI cannot approve itself or bypass the business owner.
5
Outcome evidence closes the loopNo provider evidence means no claim that revenue was recovered.
Authority chain: facts → deterministic detector → deterministic Policy → bounded AI ranking/explanation → human approval → bounded execution → provider evidence → Outcome.
No production payment or production revenue claim is made by this sandbox. No PAN, CVV, OTP, payment secret or unnecessary customer PII is sent to the model.
